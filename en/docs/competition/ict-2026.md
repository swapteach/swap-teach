This is the main option for the course's "external competition (15%)". Scoring is in [docs/course/grading.md](../course/grading.md#external-competition-scoring).

## Entry rules

- Each team may enter at most 2 tracks, one from each of two different categories (we chose 大會專題類 + 指定專題類).
- 資訊應用組: max 3 teams per school from Information Management departments (we're CSIE, so this doesn't apply).
- The 鈦坦 Agile Development special award is exempt from these limits.

### All tracks
